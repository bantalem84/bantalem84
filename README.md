### Dr. Bantalem Derseh Wale

Associate Professor of Applied Linguistics (TEFL) & ELIC Coordinator at Injibara University, Ethiopia.

#### Research Focus
- AI in Education: AI-mediated speaking instruction, digital pedagogy, & educational tech evaluation.
- Corpus Linguistics: Text-and-speech alignment, parallel corpora building, & low-resource language processing.
- Language Documentation: Native language preservation and multimodal educational corpora (e.g., Awigna Multimodal Educational Corpus - AMEC).
